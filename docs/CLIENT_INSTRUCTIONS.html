1. Overview1. 概述

What is NEXA?NEXA 是什么？

NEXA Platform is an online shopping system where each store has its own website.

NEXA 平台是一个在线购物系统，每家店铺都有独立的网店。

  • One main website (https://www.nexa-mm.com/) helps people find stores and run the platform.一个主站（https://www.nexa-mm.com/）用于发现店铺并管理平台。
  • Each vendor has a separate shop link — like an independent mini-store.每位商家拥有独立的店铺链接，类似独立小店。
  • Customers shop one store at a time. There is no single mixed marketplace catalog.顾客一次只逛一家店，没有混合所有商家的统一商城目录。

Platform catalog model: Super admin maintains one platform product catalog (prices, stock, images). Vendors select products from that catalog and organize them into store categories — they do not create or edit catalog data.

平台商品目录模式：超级管理员维护统一商品目录（价格、库存、图片等）。商家从目录中选品并分配到店铺分类 — 不能创建或编辑目录数据。

Where customers shop顾客在哪里购物

Customers do not shop from a product list on the main homepage. The main site shows platform information, a vendor logo carousel (click to open a store), and links to apply or sign in.

顾客不会在主站首页的商品列表中购物。主站展示平台信息、商家 Logo 轮播（点击进店）以及入驻/登录入口。

To buy something:

如何购买：

  1. Open a vendor's store link (e.g. https://gogo.nexa-mm.com/) or click a vendor logo on the homepage carousel.打开商家店铺链接（如 https://gogo.nexa-mm.com/），或点击首页轮播中的商家 Logo。

Each store sells only its own products.

每家店只销售本店商品。

Account and profile账户与个人资料

登录的好处：订单记录、保存地址、跨设备同步购物车与收藏。

Forgot password: email → OTP code → new password. Check spam if email is delayed.

忘记密码：邮箱 → 验证码 → 新密码。邮件延迟请检查垃圾邮件。

Checkout结账

  1. Cart → Checkout购物车 → 结账
  2. Shipping address and phone收货地址与电话
  3. Delivery region / township and delivery partner (required before payment; still required when shipping is free)配送区域/乡镇及配送方式（付款前必选；免运费时也需选择）
  4. Free shipping: all qualifying items → FREE (0 MMK); delivery partner locked to free shipping免运费：全部符合免运费商品时显示 免费（0 缅元）；配送方式锁定为免运费
  5. Cash on Delivery (COD) or KBZPay — COD only when your region/partner supports it货到付款 或 KBZPay — 货到付款仅在区域/物流支持时显示
  6. Review and Place Order / Pay with KBZPay确认后下单或KBZPay 支付

Cash on Delivery货到付款

Order created immediately; pay cash on delivery. Track under Profile → Orders.

订单立即创建；收货时现金支付。可在个人资料 → 订单中跟踪。

KBZPayKBZPay 支付

  • Desktop: pay via KBZPay QR on checkout桌面端：结账页 KBZPay 二维码
  • Mobile: pay via KBZPay app / PWA手机端：KBZPay 应用 / PWA
  • After payment you may land on platform apex /summary (e.g. https://nexa-apex.online/summary) — tap Continue Shopping支付后可能跳转到平台主站 /summary — 点继续购物

Keep the browser open until payment finishes. Contact the store with receipt if payment succeeded but no order appears.

支付完成前请勿关闭浏览器。若已扣款但无订单，请联系商家并提供凭证。

Profile: Orders, Addresses, Account settings.

个人资料：订单、地址、账户设置。

聊天；电话菜单中的拨打或 Viber。联系时请提供订单号、店铺名称及支付截图。

  • Android (Chrome): Add to Home above chat.Android：点击聊天上方的添加到主屏幕。
  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen (must use Safari).iPhone：分享 → 添加到主屏幕（须使用 Safari）。

Language语言

店铺前台：英语与缅甸语。管理后台：英语与中文（不影响顾客前台语言）。

Commission rates佣金比例

Default platform commission is 0%. The platform takes nothing unless an admin sets a rate.

平台默认佣金为 0%。除非管理员设置比例，否则平台不收取佣金。

Level层级Set by设置方Default默认
Vendor contract商家合同Super admin on vendor profile超级管理员在商家资料中0%
Product商品Super admin on product form超级管理员在商品表单中Blank = vendor contract, then 0%留空 = 商家合同，否则 0%
Specific product特定商品Admin enters a % on the product管理员在商品上填写百分比e.g. 15% on that product only例如仅该商品 15%

Resolution order per order line: line snapshot → product rate (if set) → vendor contract → 0%.

每条订单明细的优先级：行快照 → 商品比例（如已设置）→ 商家合同 → 0%。

Finances and KBZPay withdrawal财务与 KBZPay 提现

Under Finances you can:

在财务模块可以：

  • View revenue and commission查看收入与佣金
  • See available balance eligible for withdrawal查看可提现余额
  • Save your KBZPay phone number (Myanmar 09… format)保存 KBZPay 手机号（缅甸 09… 格式）
  • Withdraw to KBZPay when balance meets the minimum余额达到最低金额后可提现至 KBZPay

Withdrawable earnings include:

可提现收入包括：

  • Orders in ready-to-ship, fulfilled, shipped, or delivered status with payment collected状态为待发货、已完成、已发货、已送达且已收款的订单
  • COD: only after delivery (or marked paid)货到付款：送达后（或标记已付）
  • KBZPay: only after payment confirmedKBZPay：支付确认后

Before withdrawing:

提现前：

  1. You must be signed in (session token from login)必须已登录（登录后获得会话令牌）
  2. If you see "Session expired", sign out and sign in again若提示“会话已过期”，请退出后重新登录
  3. Confirm or save your KBZPay phone, then tap Withdraw now确认或保存 KBZPay 手机号后点击立即提现

Products and categories商品与分类

Super admin creates all catalog products (name, price, stock, images, SKU, status). Vendors only choose which items appear in their store.

超级管理员创建所有目录商品（名称、价格、库存、图片、SKU、状态）。商家仅选择本店上架的商品。

Products tab商品模块

  1. Select Product → browse platform catalog → check items → Save (same item can be sold by multiple vendors)选择商品 → 浏览平台目录 → 勾选 → 保存（同一商品可被多个商家销售）
  2. Price, stock, and status are read-only in the list列表中价格、库存、状态为只读
  3. Remove from store unlinks from your shop (product stays in platform catalog)从店铺移除取消本店关联（商品仍保留在平台目录）
  4. View opens read-only product details查看打开只读详情

Categories tab分类模块

  1. Create a vendor category (name, cover, description)创建店铺分类（名称、封面、描述）
  2. Assign products already selected for your store分配已在本店选中的商品
  3. Categories become tabs on your storefront分类显示为前台分类标签
  4. Super-admin product categories are not imported — use your own vendor categories不会导入超管商品分类 — 请使用商家自建分类

Typical workflow: Select Product → create category → assign products → preview store.

典型流程：选择商品 → 创建分类 → 分配商品 → 预览店铺。

Free shipping (if enabled): per-product on Products, or bulk by category on Products / Categories.

免运费（如已开启）：在商品中按单品，或在商品/分类中按分类批量设置。

Orders订单

Update status: Pending → Processing → Ready to ship → Fulfilled (shipping may show Shipped / Delivered). COD: prepare and collect cash. KBZPay: verify payment before shipping. Use the KBZPay draft recovery panel if a customer paid but no order was created.

更新状态：待处理 → 处理中 → 待发货 → 已完成。货到付款：备货并收货款。KBZPay：确认到账后再发货。若顾客已付但无订单，使用 KBZPay 草稿恢复 面板。

Settings and sharing设置与推广

Configure logo, banner, phone, subdomain, custom domain, terms, and social links. Share your store URL, not the main site product list. Use preview / open store in admin to verify catalog, categories, checkout, and scroll restore.

配置 Logo、横幅、电话、子域名、自定义域名、条款与社交链接。分享本店链接，而非主站商品列表。在后台使用预览/打开店铺验证目录、分类、结账与滚动恢复。

4. Super admin guide4. 超级管理员指南

Access访问

Open https://www.nexa-mm.com/admin and sign in. Left menu (role-dependent): Home, Product (Products, Categories, Inventory), Orders, Vendor, Chat, Customers, Subscriptions (Plans, Subscribers), Finances, Logistics, Settings. Marketing is not in the current menu.

打开 https://www.nexa-mm.com/admin 并登录。左侧菜单（因角色而异）：首页、商品、订单、商家、聊天、顾客、订阅、财务、物流、设置。营销不在当前菜单中。

Only Store Owner can add staff under Settings → Users.

Vendors商家管理

  • New sellers apply at /vendor/application; approve via Vendor → Review applications.新商家在 /vendor/application 申请；通过商家 → 审核入驻批准。
  • There is no "Add vendor" button — all vendors come through the application flow.没有“添加商家”按钮 — 所有商家均通过入驻流程。
  • Free shipping feature access — toggle per vendor to enable/disable free-shipping controls in vendor admin.免运费功能权限 — 按商家开关，控制商家后台是否显示免运费设置。

Platform product catalog (super admin)平台商品目录（超级管理员）

Under Products and Inventory, super admin creates and edits the platform catalog — price, stock, images, SKU, status, vendor assignment, and product commission rates. Vendors pick from this pool; they cannot change price or stock.

在 Products 与 Inventory 中，超级管理员创建并编辑平台目录 — 价格、库存、图片、SKU、状态、商家分配及商品佣金。商家从目录选品，不能修改价格或库存。

Vendor commission (super admin)商家佣金（超级管理员）

When creating or editing a vendor, set Commission %. Default is 0% when unset. Product-level commission overrides are set by super admin on individual products in the platform catalog.

创建或编辑商家时设置佣金 %。未设置时默认为 0%。商品级佣金覆盖由超级管理员在平台目录的商品表单中设置。

Daily workflow日常工作

  1. Review Orders (pending badge)查看订单（待处理角标）
  2. Recover KBZPay drafts if needed — paid checkouts that never became orders必要时恢复 KBZPay 草稿订单 — 已付但未生成订单的结账
  3. Vendor → Review applications商家 → 审核入驻申请
  4. Settings → Activities audit log设置 → 活动 审计日志

Do's and don'ts注意事项

Do建议

  • Use the correct URL for your role使用对应角色的正确链接

Don't避免
